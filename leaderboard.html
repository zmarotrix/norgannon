How it counts

Fixes

Corrections your finds helped put into NorgannonDB. If you confirm something another player saw first, it counts for you too. Fixes decide your place.

Finds

Everything your Collector noticed was different from the game data, fixed yet or not.

Your name, your choice

Players are anonymous unless they ask to be shown when they share. Pick any name: it doesn't have to be your character's. Share again as anonymous and your name comes off.

Nothing counts twice

Each time you share, your new file replaces your last one.
