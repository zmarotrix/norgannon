How it counts

Points

Each fix your finds help put into NorgannonDB earns 10 points, more if it answers a bounty. Confirming something another player saw first counts too.

Checked before it counts

Every file is checked when it arrives, and a change to the game data needs more than one player behind it. Only fixes that make it into an update earn points.

Your name, your choice

Players are anonymous unless they ask to be shown when they share. Pick any name: it doesn't have to be your character's. Share again as anonymous and your name comes off.

Nothing counts twice

Each time you share, your new file replaces your last one.
