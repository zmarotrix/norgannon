Why it matters

Classic's data is wrong in WoW Forever

Forever moved creatures, changed what bosses drop and reworked professions. Add-ons that copied their data from Classic quietly send you to the wrong place. Some things, like many boss drop chances, were never known at all.

Norgannon keeps one shared record of the game, checked against what players really see. Fix it once, and every add-on that uses it is right for everyone. Why we're doing this.

How it works

Four steps, and three of them are playing

  1. 1

    Install the Collector

    It's a small, free add-on with nothing to set up.

  2. 2

    Play as usual

    It quietly notes creatures, objects, quests and loot the game data has wrong or missing. It waits until after each fight.

  3. 3

    See what you found

    Each time you log in, it shows what your last session discovered.

  4. 4

    Share it

    Drop your Collector file on the share page and post it. Your finds go into the next update for every player.

Private by design

The Collector never records character names or other players, and nothing leaves your computer until you choose to share it.

No effect on your game

It never shows a window while you play, and it saves its work after combat so it can't slow a fight.

Open to everyone

The game data is free and open. Any add-on can use it, not only Norgannon's.

For add-on authors

Use the same data in your own add-on

NorgannonDB is an ordinary add-on with a written, stable format, free under the GPL-3.0. Load it from your add-on instead of carrying your own copy of the game.
