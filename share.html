Share your finds

Three steps, about ten seconds

Your finds go from the game straight into our Discord. No files to look for, and no forms to fill in.

  1. 1

    In the game

    Type /ndbc share, then press Ctrl + C.

  2. 2

    In our Discord

    Open #share-finds, press Ctrl + V, then Enter.

  3. 3

    That's it

    Your post disappears, and our bot tells you privately what you found. Your finds go into the next update for every player.

Join our DiscordNot on our Discord yet? It's free, and you only need to join once.

You stay anonymous unless you choose otherwise. After your first share, the bot asks you privately whether to show your name on the leaderboard, and you can change it any time with /credit in Discord. Maro can still see which Discord account sent finds, so no one is counted twice. Shared finds are checked with AI help, then Maro approves each change.

Other ways to send finds

No Discord? You can send your Collector file from this page instead. It takes a few more steps and a free GitHub account. Your file is read here in your browser, and nothing is sent until you post it yourself.

1

Find your Collector file

Log out or type /reload first, so the game saves it. Then open this folder:

World of Warcraft\_forever_\WTF\Account\YOUR ACCOUNT\SavedVariables

The file is called NorgannonDBCollector.lua. It holds only what the Collector saw in the world: no character names, and nothing about other players.

0finds
0creatures
0objects
0quests
0items
0sessions

3

Post it

Your browser saved norgannon-finds.txt. Open the form, drag that file into the box, and press Create. You'll need a free GitHub account.

Share again whenever you like. Each new file replaces your last one, so nothing counts twice.
