Questions

Before you install

Still wondering about something? Ask in #help on our Discord.

Are they free?

Yes. Every Norgannon add-on is free to download and use.

Do I need all of them?

No. Install only the ones you want. Each one also needs Norgannon Core, a small shared add-on, and some need NorgannonDB, the game data. The add-ons page says which.

Will they work with my other add-ons?

Usually, yes. If you run two add-ons that do the same job, like two quest helpers or two bag add-ons, keep the one you prefer. Clairvoyance even shows the quest progress of party members who use Questie.

Will they slow my game down?

They're built to stay light, and they hold off on any extra work until a fight is over. Add-ons that don't need the game data don't load it.

Do they play the game for me?

No. They show you information. Anything they do for you, like selling junk at a vendor, is off until you switch it on, and every heal, roll and whisper is a click you make.

What does the Collector record?

Creatures, objects, quests and loot it sees while you play, and only what the game data has wrong or missing. Never character names and never other players. You choose when to share it.

Something's broken. Where do I tell you?

These are early versions, so reports help a lot. Post in #bug-reports on our Discord, or open an issue on the add-on's GitHub page, and say what you were doing.

Can I support the work?

Everything here is free, and it stays free. If you'd like to say thanks, you can buy Maro a coffee at ko-fi.com/zmarotrix. Sharing your finds helps just as much.
